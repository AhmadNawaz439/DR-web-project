Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so we can reduce your waiting time. Message us on WhatsApp or Facebook to book.

Are female doctors available?

Yes. Our clinic is led by Dr Amna, and female doctors are available for skin, aesthetics and gynae consultations.

Can my whole family be seen at one visit?

Yes. With physicians, pediatricians, dental, gynae and more under one roof, you can book multiple consultations in the same visit.

Is there a pharmacy at the clinic?

Yes. Our in-house pharmacy is on the ground floor, so you can collect your medicines and skincare before you leave.

How do I find out about the latest deals?

Follow our Facebook page for monthly offers, or message us on WhatsApp to ask about current packages.
